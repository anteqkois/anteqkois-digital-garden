Inversion Fair Value Gaps that appear after [sweeps of liquidity](https://howtotrade.com/blog/liquidity-sweep/) tend to have a higher chance of holding the price. Liquidity, in this sense, can be in the form of:

- Daily highs/lows
- Session highs/lows
- multiple equal tops/bottoms
- Support and resistance zones
- or any lows and highs sticking out.
